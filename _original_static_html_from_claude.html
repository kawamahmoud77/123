What We Do

Plastering & Skimming Services

Small repairs to full house projects — everything done properly, left clean, and ready for decorating.

Skimming

A smooth, thin skim coat applied over existing plaster or plasterboard — ideal for walls that need freshening up before decorating. Clean edges, flat finish.

Full Room & House Skimming

Single rooms to complete properties — walls and ceilings skimmed smooth throughout. Great for new builds, renovations, or homes being prepared for sale or redecoration.

Artex Covering

Cover over old Artex textured ceilings with a flat, smooth plastered finish — no mess, no fuss. A popular job that transforms the look of older ceilings.

Ceiling Plastering

From ceilings with minor cracks to full replastering — finished flat and ready for paint. All work protected and cleaned up properly before Ali leaves.

Plaster Repairs & Patch Work

Hairline cracks, damp patches, holes, or damaged sections neatly repaired and blended in. Small jobs are no problem — everything gets the same attention to detail.

Ready for Decorating

Every job is finished to decorator standard — smooth, clean, and properly cured. Ali won't rush a finish. When it's done, it's done right.
